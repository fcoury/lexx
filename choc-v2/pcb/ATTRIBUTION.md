# Expanded Choc V2 PCB sources

- The split finger and thumb boards, short flex links and wireless controllers follow the KobitoKey build guide. The 58-key layout, schematics and routing derive from this project's Expanded MX Rev B design. [KobitoKey build guide](https://note.com/11_50iii/n/n75cff4d3502c) · [KobitoKey mechanical release](https://github.com/juichi50iii/KobitoKey_QWERTY/releases/tag/v1.0.1)
- The PG1353 footprint uses the Kailh CPG135301D01 switch drawing and CPG135001S30 socket drawing. The two 3.00 mm socket bores are 5.00 × 2.20 mm apart. The 5.00 mm center bore locates the switch. The 1.65 × 2.05 mm oblong slot clears the switch's separate retention leg on both the older and revised drawings. The bottom copper lands measure 2.60 mm square. [Kailh switch drawing](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0657/6075/5954/files/SPEC-CPG135301D01_Kailh_Choc_V2_Low_Profile_Red_Switch_03cd29a8-065e-45f8-8053-b72dcd70616d.pdf?v=1667190153) · [Older Kailh drawing](https://github.com/keyboardio/keyswitch_documentation/blob/master/datasheets/Kailh/CPG135301D01.pdf) · [Kailh socket specification](https://www.kailhswitch.com/uploads/15927/files/CPG135001S30.pdf)
- The 2D socket fabrication artwork derives from ebastler's marbastlib, revision `6b0a9a73`, under CERN-OHL-P-2.0. This version uses insulated sleeve and retention holes, adjusts the lands, and adds an independently dimensioned socket envelope for the 3D viewer. The envelope approximates the exterior and terminals. [Source footprint](https://github.com/ebastler/marbastlib/blob/6b0a9a73f579e377816d60b58eac2b3252de7868/footprints/marbastlib-xp-choc.pretty/SW_choc_v2_HS_CPG135001S30_1u.kicad_mod) · [License](licenses/marbastlib-CERN-OHL-P-2.0.txt)
- The XIAO footprint derives from Seeed Studio's XIAO-nRF52840-SMD footprint, with four unused SWD/NFC test lands omitted. The adapted footprint is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. [Seeed footprint](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OPL_Kicad_Library/blob/master/Seeed%20Studio%20XIAO%20Series%20Library/XIAO-nRF52840-SMD.kicad_mod) · [License](licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt)
- The Hirose FH12, JST PH, SOD-123 and PCM12 footprints derive from the KiCad standard libraries under CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad libraries exception. The connector shield hold-downs are electrically isolated. This project supplies approximate 3D envelopes for the XIAO and the battery connector. [KiCad library license](https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/)
